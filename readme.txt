This project concerns a course in Industrial Engineering titled "Principles of Production and Inventory Planning and Control," 
and our objective is to calculate an ordering model known as the "allowed shortages, instantaneous receipt, and gradual consumption." model.

In this model, we can calculate the 
economic order quantity, 
total holding cost, 
total ordering cost, 
total inventory cost,
total shortage cost,
and Positive warehouse inventory level.




Inputs that should be received from the application as desired include 
the cost per order, 
the cost of holding a unit of product, 
the rate of product consumption, 
the amount of safety stock,
the cost unit associated with shortage.
